# What's new in Scenario Branches

One section per version, newest first. The in-Foundry "What's new" notice,
the GitHub release notes and the Boosty post all come from here.

## 0.7.0 — 2026-09-15

- **✚ Reserve — a new node kind.** A scene up your sleeve: the rescue that pulls the party out of a hopeless fight. No arrows lead to a reserve node — the GM spends it when the moment calls for it and marks it as passed, and the arrows leaving it open.
- **"Check" no longer calls a reserve node an orphan** or a dead end, nor what lies beyond it unreachable. It does point out an arrow that leads into a reserve.
- **An ending reachable only through a reserve is never declared cut off:** the GM may spend the rescue at any moment.
- **On the canvas a reserve has a dotted border;** without saved positions it is laid out in its own lane below the scenario.
- **Fixed:** forks are drawn dashed and endings with their own border, as intended. The node-kind style was not being applied before.
- **A "What's new" notice** when the world starts: after an update, and when a new version is out on GitHub. Checking for new versions can be turned off in the module settings.
- **Module language in its settings:** "Same as Foundry", "Русский" or "English". Foundry can run in English while the module speaks Russian, and the other way round.
- **With any Foundry language other than Russian the module now speaks English,** not Russian.
